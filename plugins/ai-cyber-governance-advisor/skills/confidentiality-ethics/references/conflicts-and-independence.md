# Conflicts of Interest and Independence

**Verify before reliance.** Auditor independence rules (CERT-In empanelment guidelines, SEBI CSCRF auditor requirements, RBI IS audit norms, ISO/IEC 17021-1 for certification bodies, DPDP Rules on the independent data auditor for SDFs) change. Search the current text and record the date checked.

## 1. Conflict check procedure

1. **Before** receiving confidential information or sending a proposal, collect: client legal name and group entities, ultimate owners, counterparties, adverse parties, related regulators, the matter description and the service type.
2. Search the client and matter register (current clients and those of the last 3 years, or the firm's policy period), including group entities and counterparties.
3. Ask every proposed team member to declare personal interests: relationships, shareholdings, prior employment with any party (last 2–3 years), outside roles.
4. Classify any hit (section 2) and decide (section 3).
5. Record the check before work starts. Re-run the check if parties or scope change.

## 2. Conflict types

| Type | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Direct | Acting against a current client, or for two clients whose interests are opposed in the same or a related matter | Investigating a vendor breach for the bank while advising the vendor on the same incident |
| Confidential information | Holding one client's confidential information that would be material to another client | Assessing a competitor's AI product after reviewing the first client's roadmap |
| Positional | Advocating opposite positions for different clients on the same issue | Arguing to a regulator that a rule applies to one client and not to another in the same circumstances |
| Self-review | Assessing or testifying on work the firm itself designed or implemented | Auditing a SOC the firm built |
| Personal | A team member's personal or financial interest | Team member's spouse is a director of the counterparty |
| Commercial | Fee or referral arrangement that may bias advice | Recommending a tool from which the firm receives commission without disclosure |

## 3. Decision and safeguards

| Outcome | When | Safeguards |
|---|---|---|
| No conflict | No hit, or hit is unrelated | Record the check |
| Proceed with safeguards | Risk can be reduced to an acceptable level | Informed written consent of all affected clients; ethical wall; separate teams and partners; separate repositories and access logs; disclosure of commercial interests |
| Decline / withdraw | Opposing parties in a dispute; self-review in an independent role; confidential information cannot be walled; consent refused | Decline in writing without disclosing the other client's confidential details |

**Informed consent wording (adapt):**
> "[Firm] currently advises [describe, without confidential detail] on [general nature]. We do not consider this prevents us acting for you on [matter], subject to these safeguards: [list]. Information from each engagement will not be shared with the other team. Please confirm in writing that you consent to us acting on this basis."

**Ethical wall checklist:** named team lists on each side; no shared staff; separate storage with access logging; written undertakings from staff; no discussion across the wall; periodic monitoring; a named partner responsible.

## 5. Independence threats and safeguards (IESBA-style framework)

| Threat | Typical source in cyber / privacy / AI work | Safeguards |
|---|---|---|
| Self-interest | High fee dependence on one client; contingent fees; follow-on implementation work expected from findings | Fee limits; no contingent fees for audit or assurance; separate the assessment and implementation engagements |
| Self-review | Auditing controls, policies or models the firm built or advised on | Do not accept the audit; or use a fully separate team if the rules allow it and disclose |
| Advocacy | Acting as the client's advocate (for example before the regulator) and as an independent assessor | Separate roles; decline the independent role |
| Familiarity | Long association with client management; former employee on the team | Rotation of the engagement lead; cooling-off period |
| Intimidation | Threat of losing the client; pressure to soften findings | Escalation route to the firm's risk lead; documented finding-change log |

**Conclusion options:** independent / independent with safeguards (listed) / not independent → decline.

## 6. Role-specific notes (verify current rules)

- **CERT-In empanelled auditors:** follow the empanelment guidelines on conflict of interest, non-disclosure and data handling; an auditor should not audit systems it designed, developed or operates for the auditee.
- **SEBI CSCRF cyber audits / RBI IS audits:** check the regulator's auditor eligibility, rotation and independence conditions.
- **DPDP Significant Data Fiduciary:** the independent data auditor must be independent of the SDF; check the Rules and any Board guidance.
- **ISO certification bodies:** ISO/IEC 17021-1 forbids certifying a management system the body (or a related body) has consulted on.
- **Expert witnesses:** the overriding duty is to the court; disclose any prior advisory role for a party.
- **Forensic investigators:** avoid investigating matters in which the firm's own controls or staff are implicated.
